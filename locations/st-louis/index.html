    Planning an Event in St. Louis

    Popular venue types here include breweries, historic Downtown buildings, rooftops, loft spaces and suburban event centers. The best outdoor window is April through June and September through October. Summers are hot and humid, and winters bring cold snaps and ice. For July, August and December through February, we'll steer you toward indoor or covered options. Most guests drive, and MetroLink connects Downtown, the Central West End and Clayton.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does Hop To It Events plan events in St. Louis?

    Yes. We plan corporate events, team building, food truck catering, brand activations, festivals and grand openings across Greater St. Louis, including Downtown, Central West End, Soulard, the Grove, Lafayette Square and Clayton.

    What kinds of venues work for events in St. Louis?

    It depends on the event and season. Breweries, historic Downtown buildings, rooftops and loft spaces are common choices here, and for outdoor events we look at Forest Park, riverfront spaces and county parks. We'll recommend venue types that fit your guest count, budget and the time of year.

    How far ahead should we plan events in St. Louis?

    For most events, start 6–10 weeks out, and earlier for dates in April through June and September through October, which is peak outdoor season in St. Louis. Dates that overlap with major events at America's Center or big local weekends fill venues and hotels quickly. Share your target date and we'll tell you what's realistic.

    How does weather affect events in St. Louis?

    Summers are hot and humid, and winters bring cold snaps and ice. We plan the format around the season: outdoors in April through June and September through October, and indoor or covered options for July, August and December through February.

    What affects the cost of events in St. Louis?

    Guest count, date and season, venue type, food and beverage, entertainment and staffing are the biggest drivers. In St. Louis, peak dates in April through June and September through October and venue choice (breweries and historic downtown buildings versus a space you already have) move budgets the most.
