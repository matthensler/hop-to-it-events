Corporate Venues Around Jersey City

Companies in the Exchange Place financial district, Newport and Harborside often book waterfront venues with skyline views, rooftop terraces and loft spaces. We match the venue to your headcount, format and budget, or work with your office, campus or a space you already have.

Planning Around the Jersey City Calendar

Holiday parties fill December quickly, and outdoor company events work best in May through June and September through October. Summers are hot and humid with thunderstorms, and winters bring cold and snow, so plan shade and weather backups. PATH trains, ferries and light rail connect Jersey City to Manhattan; street parking is limited.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do you plan corporate events in the Exchange Place financial district and Newport?

    Yes. We plan corporate events across Hudson County, including Downtown, Exchange Place, Newport, Journal Square, Liberty State Park and Hoboken, at your office, a venue we help source or an off-site location.

    What venues work for corporate events in Jersey City?

    Waterfront venues with skyline views, rooftop terraces, loft spaces and restaurant buyouts are all popular here. The right fit depends on guest count, whether you want a seated dinner or a mingling format, and the season.

    When should we book a holiday party in Jersey City?

    Start in late summer or early fall. December weekends and the first half of the month book first, especially at waterfront venues with skyline views. Weeknights and January celebrations are easier to secure.

    What affects the cost of a corporate event in Jersey City?

    Guest count, date and season, venue type, food and beverage, entertainment and staffing are the biggest drivers. In Jersey City, peak dates in May through June and September through October and venue choice (waterfront venues with skyline views and rooftop terraces versus a space you already have) move budgets the most.
