Not Your Average Company Picnic.

Great food, fun activities and a reason to get everyone together. Hop To It Events creates company picnics that feel more like a celebration than another corporate obligation. Whether you're hosting a small employee gathering or a large company-wide event, we'll help bring the experience to life.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a company picnic planner do?

We find the venue or park, line up food trucks and caterers, plan games and entertainment, and manage the event on the day so your team can enjoy it.

Can families attend the company picnic?

Yes. We plan family-friendly experiences with activities and entertainment for all ages.

Can you plan a festival-style company picnic?

Yes. We can build a picnic with multiple food vendors, entertainment, interactive activities and customized experiences.

How much does a company picnic cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, location, date, the services you need and the experience you want to create. Share your approximate budget on the inquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit it.

You Bring the Team. We'll Bring the Fun.

From planning and vendor coordination to event-day execution, we help make your company picnic easy to organize and enjoyable to attend.
