FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a brand activation?

A brand activation is a live experience that lets people interact with a brand or product in person, such as a sampling program, pop-up, launch event or sponsored experience at a festival.

What's the difference between a brand activation and experiential marketing?

Experiential marketing is the overall strategy of reaching people through live experiences. A brand activation is a specific event or moment within that strategy.

Can my brand activate at one of your festivals?

Yes. Our signature events, including Donut Fest and Chef Battle, offer sponsorship and activation opportunities with food-focused audiences.

What does Hop To It handle for a brand activation?

We can coordinate the creative concept, venue, vendors, staffing, entertainment, photography and video, production and on-site execution, scaled to your objectives.

How much does a brand activation cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, location, date, the services you need and the experience you want to create. Share your approximate budget on the inquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit it.
