FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are good employee appreciation event ideas?

Food truck appreciation days, office festivals, recognition celebrations, interactive team experiences like culinary competitions, and holiday or seasonal events.

Can you host an employee appreciation event at our office?

Yes. We can turn a parking lot, courtyard or office space into a festival-style celebration, or bring food trucks directly to your workplace.

Do you plan appreciation events for a single department?

Yes. We plan events for one department or an entire company, tailored to your team, goals and budget.

What does Hop To It coordinate for an appreciation event?

Vendors, food, entertainment, activities, logistics and on-site execution, so your team can simply enjoy the day.

Big or Small, Make It Memorable

Whether you're celebrating one department or bringing your entire company together, we'll help create an experience tailored to your team, goals and budget.
