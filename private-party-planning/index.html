FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan private parties as well as corporate events?

Yes. We plan birthday, anniversary and milestone parties, graduation celebrations, family gatherings, neighborhood parties and holiday celebrations.

Can I book a food truck for a backyard party?

Yes. One truck or several, food truck parties are a popular option for birthdays, graduations and neighborhood get-togethers.

What do you need to know to plan my party?

What you're celebrating, where you're hosting, your date and how many guests you're expecting.

How much does a private party cost to plan?

Pricing depends on guest count, location, date, the services you need and the experience you want to create. Share your approximate budget on the inquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit it.
