FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Hop To It Events' signature events?

Fifteen original food and drink event brands, including Donut Fest, Chef Battle, Bartender Battle, Wine Bash, Wing Fest, Say Cheese Fest, Cocktail Shake Off and Donut 5K.

How do I buy tickets to Donut Fest or Chef Battle?

Upcoming dates are listed under Coming Up on this page, each with a link to tickets or event details.

How can my brand sponsor a festival?

Email hello@hoptoit.events with your brand, goals and the markets you're interested in, and we'll share sponsorship options.

Can I bring one of these events to my city?

Yes. We work with cities, venues and local partners to bring established event concepts like Donut Fest and Chef Battle to new markets.
