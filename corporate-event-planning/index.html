FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of corporate events does Hop To It Events plan?

We plan company celebrations, employee appreciation events, customer and client events, sales meetings and kickoffs, company picnics, conferences and receptions, and team-building experiences.

What does full-service corporate event planning include?

Depending on your event, we can handle strategy and concept, venue sourcing, vendor coordination, food and beverage, entertainment, event marketing, logistics and on-site management. You can hire us for the whole event or for specific pieces.

Can you bring one of your festivals to our company event?

Yes. Our signature formats, including Chef Battle, Donut Fest and Bartender Battle, can be scaled for a holiday party, conference, client event or company celebration.

How much does a corporate event cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, location, date, the services you need and the experience you want to create. Share your approximate budget on the inquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit it.

Where do you plan corporate events?

We've produced events in 30+ U.S. markets. Tell us your city and venue and we'll confirm what we can support there.
