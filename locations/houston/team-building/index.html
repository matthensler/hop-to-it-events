Team-building feature

Chef Battle, Built for Your Team

Chef Battle is the live culinary competition we produce in 20+ cities with the Global Chef League. For companies, we scale it into a team cook-off: groups collaborate, create dishes and compete for bragging rights while everyone else cheers them on.

Explore Team Building See Chef Battle Events

Indoor-Friendly, Any Time of Year

Summers are hot and humid with frequent afternoon storms, and hurricane season runs June through November. Chef Battle and Bartender Battle run indoors, so they work at your office, a hotel ballroom or a rooftop terrace any time of year.

On-Site or Off-Site Around Houston

We bring the competition to offices in Downtown Houston, the Galleria and Uptown, and the Texas Medical Center, or run it off-site with a venue, open bar and passed apps. Houston is spread out and car-first; distances between Downtown, the Galleria, the Energy Corridor and The Woodlands are long, so central venues and parking matter.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Can team building happen indoors in Houston?

    Yes. Our culinary and cocktail competitions are built to run indoors, which helps from June through September, and we can move outdoors in October through April.

    Can you run team building at our Houston office?

    Yes. We set up at offices across Greater Houston, including Downtown, Midtown, Montrose, the Heights and Museum District, as long as there's room for team stations. We'll confirm space and power needs in advance.

    Can it work for large groups?

    Yes. On-site packages fit groups up to about 50, and our private-event format scales to hundreds of guests with a live pro chef competition, MC and AV.

    Is there a non-alcoholic option?

    Yes. Bartender Battle runs as a mocktail competition, and Chef Battle doesn't require alcohol at all.
