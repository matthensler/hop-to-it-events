    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What does Hop To It Events do?

    Hop To It Events is a full-service event planning and production company that has been planning and producing events since 2010. It plans corporate events, brand activations, team building, festivals, fundraisers, food truck catering and private parties, and creates its own signature food and drink festivals in 30+ U.S. markets.

    What types of events does Hop To It Events plan?

    Corporate events, employee appreciation events, company picnics, team building, brand activations, trade show activations, grand openings and product launches, festivals and community events, fundraisers, private parties and food truck catering.

    How is Hop To It different from other event planners?

    We've been planning and producing events since 2010, and we don't just plan events, we create our own. Our team builds, markets, tickets and produces signature event brands like Chef Battle and Donut Fest, and we bring that experience to every client event.

    Where does Hop To It Events produce events?

    We've produced events in 30+ U.S. markets, including Los Angeles, San Diego, Las Vegas, Chicago, Austin, Denver, Atlanta and Boston. Tell us your city and we'll confirm what we can support.

    How do I start planning an event with Hop To It?

    Fill out the Plan Your Event form with your event type, date, location, guest count and budget, or email hello@hoptoit.events. Our team reviews every inquiry and follows up to talk through the details.
