Hop To It Events Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Hop To It Events ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose personal information when you visit our websites, register for events, submit an inquiry or application, complete a social media lead form, or otherwise interact with us.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact information: Your name, email address, phone number, city, and state.
  • Business and application information: Your company name, website, social media profiles, professional experience, preferred event market, and information you provide when applying to become an operator, vendor, chef, sponsor, or event partner.
  • Event and transaction information: Registrations, ticket purchases, order details, attendance information, and communications about an event.
  • Communications: Messages, survey responses, feedback, and other information you choose to share.
  • Website usage information: IP address, browser and device information, pages visited, referring websites, and interactions with our websites or advertisements.

Ticketing platforms, payment processors, social media platforms, and other service providers may collect information directly under their own privacy policies.

How We Use Information

We may use personal information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Process registrations and coordinate events.
  • Review operator applications and partnership opportunities.
  • Communicate with chefs, vendors, sponsors, venues, and local operators.
  • Send information about events and opportunities, subject to applicable consent requirements and your communication preferences.
  • Improve our websites, events, services, and advertising.
  • Prevent fraud, protect our business, and comply with legal obligations.

Facebook, Instagram, and Other Lead Forms

When you submit a form through Facebook, Instagram, or another advertising platform, we receive the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to your request, evaluate your interest or qualifications, and discuss relevant events or partnership opportunities.

Submitting an inquiry does not guarantee selection as an operator, vendor, chef, sponsor, or partner. The platform through which you submit the form also handles information according to its own privacy policy.

How We Share Information

We may disclose personal information to:

  • Service providers that support website hosting, ticketing, payments, communications, customer management, analytics, and other business operations.
  • Event partners and local operators when necessary to fulfill your registration, respond to your request, or coordinate an event or partnership.
  • Advertising and analytics providers, where we use their tools to measure campaign performance or deliver relevant advertising.
  • Legal and professional advisers or authorities when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.
  • Parties to a business transaction, such as a merger, acquisition, or transfer of business assets, subject to applicable protections.
  • Other recipients at your direction or with your consent.

Some advertising-related disclosures may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under applicable state privacy laws, even when no money is exchanged. Any applicable opt-out rights are described below.

Cookies and Advertising Technologies

Our websites may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, understand visitor activity, and measure advertising performance. Depending on the tools enabled, advertising providers may use information about your activity to personalize advertising.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and advertising preferences through the relevant platform. Blocking cookies may affect some website features. Where applicable law requires additional consent or opt-out controls, those requirements apply to our use of these technologies.

Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails using the unsubscribe link provided. You may still receive communications necessary to complete a transaction or manage an existing event registration or partnership.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, or to opt out of certain uses or disclosures, including targeted advertising or the sale or sharing of personal information.

To make a request, contact hello@hoptoit.events. We may need to verify your identity before processing a request. Applicable legal exceptions may limit certain requests. Where required by law, you may use an authorized agent, appeal a decision, and exercise your rights without unlawful discrimination.

Retention and Security

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including managing events and partnerships, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations.

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Our websites and business inquiry forms are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information online directly from children under 13. Parents or guardians should manage registrations for children attending events. If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact hello@hoptoit.events.

Third-Party Websites

Our websites may link to ticketing services, venues, sponsors, social media platforms, and other third-party websites. Their information practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our practices change. We will post the revised policy with an updated effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent when required by law.
