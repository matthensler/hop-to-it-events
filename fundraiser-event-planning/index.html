FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What kinds of fundraising events does Hop To It plan?

Charity food festivals, benefit events and celebrations, culinary competitions, community fundraisers and corporate giving events.

Do you work with nonprofits and community groups?

Yes. We work with nonprofits, charitable organizations and community groups to develop an event concept, find vendors and partners, and coordinate production.

Can a chef competition be part of a fundraiser?

Yes. A live chef competition adds an interactive, ticketable experience that gives supporters a reason to attend.

How do we get started?

Tell us about your cause, audience, timing and goals on the inquiry form, and we'll follow up to talk through ideas.
