FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How does food truck catering work with Hop To It Events?

Tell us your date, location, guest count and food preferences. We coordinate the trucks, vendor availability and logistics, and the trucks arrive ready to serve your guests.

How many food trucks do I need?

It depends on your guest count, how long service runs and how much variety you want. Share your headcount on the quote form and we'll recommend a lineup.

What kinds of food trucks can you book?

Tacos, BBQ, burgers, pizza, wings, ice cream, coffee, desserts and more. We match the trucks to your audience, event style and budget.

Can you book food trucks for an office lunch?

Yes. Office and employee lunches are one of our most common requests, from a single appreciation-day truck to a rotating lineup.

Where do you provide food truck catering?

We've produced events in 30+ U.S. markets. Tell us your city and venue and we'll confirm what we can support there.
