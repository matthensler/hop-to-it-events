FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who does Hop To It Events plan festivals for?

Cities, chambers of commerce, tourism organizations, shopping centers, business districts, property managers and private organizations.

Can you bring Donut Fest or Chef Battle to our city?

Yes. Our signature event brands can be brought to new cities and venues. Tell us about your community and we'll discuss the right format and operating model.

What festival planning services do you provide?

Event strategy, venue selection, vendor recruitment, sponsorship development, ticketing, marketing, permitting coordination, logistics and on-site management.

How far in advance should we start planning a festival?

Public events involve permits, vendors and sponsors, so earlier is better. Reach out as soon as you have a target date and we'll map out a timeline.

Where do you produce festivals?

We've produced events in 30+ U.S. markets. Tell us your city and venue and we'll confirm what we can support there.
