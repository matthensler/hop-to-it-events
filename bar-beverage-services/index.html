FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Who serves alcohol at events you plan?

Alcohol service is provided by licensed beverage service partners where required. We coordinate the concept, vendors and logistics.

What is Bartender Battle?

Bartender Battle is a live cocktail or mocktail competition that adds creativity and friendly competition to company parties, festivals and team-building events.

Can you provide non-alcoholic beverage options?

Yes. Mocktail stations, coffee, tea and specialty beverage stations are all options.

What do you need to know to plan a beverage experience?

Your event type, guest count, location and the kind of beverage experience you have in mind.
