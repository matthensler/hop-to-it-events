FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Chef Battle team-building event?

Chef Battle is a live culinary competition. Teams collaborate to create dishes and compete for bragging rights, with the format adapted to your group and venue.

Where can a team-building event take place?

At your office, a conference venue or an off-site location. We coordinate the format, materials, vendors and production for the space you choose.

Do you offer non-alcoholic options for Bartender Battle?

Yes. Bartender Battle can be run as a cocktail or mocktail competition.

Can you customize a team-building activity for our company?

Yes. We build custom team competitions around your group size, company culture and event goals.

How much does corporate team building cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, location, date, the services you need and the experience you want to create. Share your approximate budget on the inquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit it.
