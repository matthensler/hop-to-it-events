About Hop To It Events

Big Ideas. Unforgettable Experiences.

Hop To It Events is a full-service event planning and production company creating memorable experiences for brands, businesses, organizations and communities. We’ve been planning and executing events since 2010.

From corporate celebrations and brand activations to food festivals and large-scale public events, we bring creativity, expertise and hands-on execution to every experience we produce.

Our Passion

We believe great events bring people together, create meaningful connections and leave lasting impressions.

Our Approach

Every event starts with a great idea. We combine creative planning, strategic partnerships and hands-on production to bring that idea to life.

Our Experience

We’ve been planning and executing events since 2010. Having produced events across the country, we understand what it takes to create engaging experiences, attract audiences, coordinate partners and execute successfully.

Why Choose Hop To It

We’re Event Creators, Not Just Event Planners

We’ve developed and produced our own signature event brands, giving us firsthand experience in every stage of event planning, marketing and execution.

Creative Ideas. Real-World Execution.

From sourcing venues and coordinating vendors to managing logistics and event-day production, we know how to turn ambitious concepts into successful experiences.

Built Around Your Goals

No two events are the same. We tailor every experience to your audience, objectives and budget, whether you’re planning a private gathering or a large-scale public event.
