FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you plan business anniversary celebrations?

Yes. We plan milestone anniversary events for businesses, from customer appreciation parties to community celebrations with food and entertainment.

What makes a successful grand opening event?

A clear reason for people to show up and a chance to experience the business firsthand: food, entertainment, interactive moments and a well-run guest experience.

Do you plan restaurant openings?

Yes. We plan openings for restaurants, bars and hospitality concepts, as well as retail stores and other businesses.

Can you help with a product launch?

Yes. We plan product launch events with demonstrations, sampling and experiential activations.

How much does a grand opening event cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, location, date, the services you need and the experience you want to create. Share your approximate budget on the inquiry form and we'll recommend options that fit it.
