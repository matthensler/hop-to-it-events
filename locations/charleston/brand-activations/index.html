Where to Activate in Charleston

High-foot-traffic districts like King Street, the French Quarter and Park Circle suit sampling and pop-ups, subject to property and city approvals. For B2B audiences, tourism and hospitality, and aerospace events and trade shows are strong options.

Staffing, Logistics and Season

Outdoor activations perform best in March through June and September through November. Summers are hot and humid and hurricane season runs June through November, so have a rain and weather plan. We handle booth setup, staffing, supplies and recap reporting.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What kinds of brand activations do you run in Charleston?

    Product sampling, pop-ups, product launches, festival activations and trade show programming, from a single staffed table to multi-day tours.

    Can you staff a sampling program in Charleston?

    Yes. We provide trained brand ambassadors and on-site managers, priced by days and hours on site, and can run activations around Downtown, Upper King, French Quarter and Mount Pleasant.

    Can my brand activate at one of your festivals?

    Our signature events, including Donut Fest and Chef Battle, offer sponsorship and activation opportunities. Ask which events are scheduled near your audience.

    How far ahead should we plan a brand activation in Charleston?

    For most brand activations, start 6–10 weeks out, and earlier for dates in March through June and September through November, which is peak outdoor season in Charleston. Popular weekends and holiday dates fill quickly. Share your target date and we'll tell you what's realistic.
